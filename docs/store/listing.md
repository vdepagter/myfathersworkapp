# Store listing texts

Three description variants for the Microsoft Store (also usable for Google Play's full description, max 4,000
characters there). Pick one; the disclaimer paragraph at the end of each is needed in every variant.

## A - Story first

The family business was never meant to be kind.

My Father's Work (Unofficial) is a free, fan-made companion app for the board game My Father's Work. Put a phone,
tablet or laptop next to the board and let the app run the story: it tells you what to set up each round, presents the
events, votes and secret choices, and narrates every page as three generations of your family chase their father's
dreadful ambitions.

- All three scenarios: The Cost of Disease, Fear of the Unknown and A Time of War, with every branch and all 8 endings
  of each
- Fully narrated by Oliver, with the sentence being read gently lit on the page, or the original male and female
  recordings
- Works offline: everything, including all narration, is part of the app
- Go back and forward through every screen, keep several games side by side under Saved Games, and continue where you
  left off
- An Endings Gallery with achievements and a Hall of Fame, a Log Book of the events so far, and an optional Guided mode
  for hunting every ending
- For 2 to 4 players

You need the board game My Father's Work to play. This is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed
by Renegade Game Studios. My Father's Work, its stories, artwork, music and original voice recordings are © Renegade
Game Studios.

## B - Practical

A free companion app for the board game My Father's Work, made by players for players.

The app replaces the official companion app at the table: it guides 2 to 4 players through all three scenarios
(The Cost of Disease, Fear of the Unknown and A Time of War) over three Generations of three rounds, with the setup for
every event, the votes and bids, the secret pages and the final scoring.

What you get:
- Every scenario complete, with all branches and all 8 endings of each
- Narration of all story text by Oliver, or the original recordings
- Everything works without an internet connection, narration included
- Undo and redo for every screen, saved games, and automatic saving after every screen
- Endings Gallery, achievements, Hall of Fame and a Log Book
- Guided mode (spoilers!) that shows which choices lead where
- Adjustable story text size, volumes and graphics, and full screen with F11

No account, no ads, no purchases, and no data collected: your games stay on your device.

You need the board game My Father's Work to play. This is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed
by Renegade Game Studios. My Father's Work, its stories, artwork, music and original voice recordings are © Renegade
Game Studios.

## Notes for certification (Microsoft Store)

Desktop app packaged as MSIX. It needs runFullTrust because it serves its bundled files on localhost inside its own
process and shows them in a WebView2 window; it makes no other network connections. This is a non-commercial fan
project; the original companion app's source was published by Renegade Game Studios under the Creative Commons
Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 licence, and this app is shared under the same terms. No account is needed to test it:
start the app, press New Game and follow the screens.
