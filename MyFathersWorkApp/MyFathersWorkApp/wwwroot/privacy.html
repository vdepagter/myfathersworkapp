Privacy

My Father's Work (Unofficial) - last updated 22 November 2026

My Father's Work (Unofficial) is a free, non-commercial fan-made companion app for the board game My Father's Work. It is available as a website, as a Windows app (a download and in the Microsoft Store) and as an Android app. This page explains what happens with your information in each of them. In short: the app collects nothing about you, and everything you enter stays on your own device.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your games (player names, the village name, choices, scores and the undo history), saved games, the Endings Gallery and your settings are kept in the storage of the browser or app on your device.
  • Narration you download for offline play is kept in the same local storage.
  • None of this is sent anywhere. It leaves your device only when you choose to: with a save code or a gallery export that you share yourself.
  • You can remove it at any time: remove the downloads in Offline play, clear the site's data in your browser, or uninstall the app.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no in-app purchases.
  • No analytics, tracking, crash reporting or cookies of its own.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files beyond saving the files you ask it to save.

Network use

  • Website and Android app: the app files and narration are downloaded from GitHub Pages (vdepagter.github.io), which hosts the site. As with any website, GitHub receives the technical information needed to deliver the files, such as your IP address. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Windows app: all app files and narration are included. The app runs a small web server that is only reachable from your own computer (localhost) and shows the app in its own window. It needs no internet connection. Links to other websites, such as the source code, open in your browser.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

Changes and contact

If this changes, this page will be updated first. Questions, bug reports and ideas: github.com/vdepagter/myfathersworkapp/issues.
