# Changelog

What changed in each version of My Father's Work (Unofficial), newest first. The version number is shown at the
bottom of Help, Settings and the pause menu. Shown in the app under Help > What's new.

## v0.66.6.102 - 21 November 2026

- The app is now called "My Father's Work (Unofficial)" (without "App"), on the website, as an installed app, on
  Android and in the Windows download.

## v0.66.6.101 - 21 November 2026

- Android app (APK to install yourself): the whole app with music and sound, about 35 MB. Narration is downloaded in
  the app under Offline play (asked on the first start) and otherwise plays from the internet. The phone's Back button
  closes panels, opens the pause menu in a game and puts the app away on the Main Menu.

## v0.66.6.104 - 21 November 2026

- Windows: the app now runs in its own window instead of opening Microsoft Edge alongside it. Saved games, the Endings
  Gallery and settings from the previous version are carried over on the first start.

## v0.66.6.103 - 21 November 2026

- Oliver's narration is half the size: about 55-70 MB per scenario to download for offline play, instead of 110-140 MB.
  It is encoded again from the original recordings. Narration you downloaded before is fetched once more.
- The Windows download is code-signed, and is now about 270 MB. The separate light download is gone, because the full
  one is now about that size.

## v0.66.6.100 - 21 November 2026

- One Offline play menu, opened from Settings, the pause menu and the "Offline play" button on the Main Menu (was
  "Install app"). Tick the narration you want on this device and press Download: unticked narration that was
  downloaded is removed. Oliver's three scenarios are ticked to start with, the original voices are not.

## v0.66.6.99 - 21 November 2026

- Firefox: the app now gets ready for offline use there too (it stopped at the music files before).
- Right after installing the app, or on its first start as an app, it asks which narration to download for offline
  play (everything is ticked; untick what you don't want).
- Settings > Play offline: Download all, Remove all downloads to free up space, and how to install from Firefox's menu
  on Android.

## v0.66.6.98 - 21 November 2026

- The website works offline: after the first visit the app is kept on your device, and it can be installed as an app
  with the "Install app" button on the Main Menu (on iPhone and iPad it shows how), in Settings > Play offline, or
  with the browser's own install button.
- Narration for offline play: Settings > Play offline downloads Oliver per scenario or the original voices. Downloads
  stay through app updates; only changed clips are fetched again.
- A new version shows "A new version of the app is ready" with an Update button, and "Updated to ..." afterwards.

## v0.66.6.97 - 16 November 2026

- Long story pages get a "↓ Continue" shortcut (or "↓ Reveal", "↓ Final Scoring", ...) that jumps down to the page's
  button, like "↓ End of Round" on the round screens.
- New heading and button fonts (Frijole and Bowlby One SC). The original app's fonts were licensed for personal use
  only; these are free to share.
- Help: a What's new page (this list) and credits under This app.
- Release downloads: besides the full Windows version, a smaller one with lighter audio and one without any audio.

## v0.66.6.89 - v0.66.6.96 - 15 and 16 November 2026

- Every story page ends in a clear green button: Continue, Reveal, Start Voting, Start Bidding, Final Scoring.
- Votes and bids: Accept after the 3-2-1 countdown goes straight on to the result.
- Guided mode (spoilers!), in Settings and the pause menu: shows which choices lead to which branch or ending, and
  which endings you have already found.
- More ways to mark the sentence Oliver is speaking; "Ember by candlelight" is the new default.
- Play along with the official app moved into Settings as a tester option; the Scenario Language page is skipped.
- Quieter default volumes for music and effects, so Oliver is easy to follow.
- The app is now called "My Father's Work App (Unofficial)".

## v0.66.6.74 - v0.66.6.88 - 3 to 15 November 2026

- Oliver narrates all three scenarios in whole sentences, including the values that change between games.
- The sentence being spoken is highlighted and the page scrolls along with it; choose how in the pause menu.
- An offline Windows version that runs without an internet connection.
- Village name secrets now steer Generation II in every scenario (see Help).

## v0.66.6.45 - v0.66.6.73 - 30 October to 1 November 2026

- The official app's look: the manor Main Menu with rain, fog and lightning, parchment story pages, textured buttons,
  the round bar, ink-blot page transitions and the SPECIAL EVENT banner.
- Settings for graphics quality and story text size; final scores entered on one page.
- Saved Games keeps every game on the device; loading a save code moved into Saved Games.
- Rankings podium at the end of a game.
- Endings Gallery: a map of every branch and ending, achievements, and a Hall of Fame with a game sheet to correct,
  add or delete games.

## v0.66.6.17 - v0.66.6.44 - 29 and 30 October 2026

- Back and Forward work all the way to the start of the game, also after closing the page.
- Endings Gallery with remembered players.
- Help pages and the Log Book, now for all three scenarios.
- SPECIAL RULE blocks get their own pop-up.

## v0.66.6.8 - v0.66.6.16 - 27 to 29 October 2026

- Oliver narrates Fear of the Unknown; fixes from the first table play tests.

## v0.66.6.0 - v0.66.6.7 - 13 to 17 October 2026

- Music, sound effects, the original male and female voice-over and page transitions.
- A Time of War fully ported, with its music and voice-over.
- Version numbers.
