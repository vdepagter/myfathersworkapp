# Changelog

What changed in each version of My Father's Work App (Unofficial), newest first. The version number is shown at the
bottom of Help, Settings and the pause menu. Shown in the app under Help > What's new.

## v0.66.6.97 - 16 November 2026

- Long story pages get a "↓ Continue" shortcut (or "↓ Reveal", "↓ Final Scoring", ...) that jumps down to the page's
  button, like "↓ End of Round" on the round screens.
- Help: a What's new page (this list) and credits under This app.
- Release downloads: besides the full Windows version, a smaller one with lighter audio and one without any audio.

## v0.66.6.89 - v0.66.6.96 - 15 and 16 November 2026

- Every story page ends in a clear green button: Continue, Reveal, Start Voting, Start Bidding, Final Scoring.
- Votes and bids: Accept after the 3-2-1 countdown goes straight on to the result.
- Guided mode (spoilers!), in Settings and the pause menu: shows which choices lead to which branch or ending, and
  which endings you have already found.
- More ways to mark the sentence Oliver is speaking; "Ember by candlelight" is the new default.
- Play along with the official app moved into Settings as a tester option; the Scenario Language page is skipped.
- Quieter default volumes for music and effects, so Oliver is easy to follow.
- The app is now called "My Father's Work App (Unofficial)".

## v0.66.6.74 - v0.66.6.88 - 3 to 15 November 2026

- Oliver narrates all three scenarios in whole sentences, including the values that change between games.
- The sentence being spoken is highlighted and the page scrolls along with it; choose how in the pause menu.
- An offline Windows version that runs without an internet connection.
- Village name secrets now steer Generation II in every scenario (see Help).

## v0.66.6.45 - v0.66.6.73 - 30 October to 1 November 2026

- The official app's look: the manor Main Menu with rain, fog and lightning, parchment story pages, textured buttons,
  the round bar, ink-blot page transitions and the SPECIAL EVENT banner.
- Settings for graphics quality and story text size; final scores entered on one page.
- Saved Games keeps every game on the device; loading a save code moved into Saved Games.
- Rankings podium at the end of a game.
- Endings Gallery: a map of every branch and ending, achievements, and a Hall of Fame with a game sheet to correct,
  add or delete games.

## v0.66.6.17 - v0.66.6.44 - 29 and 30 October 2026

- Back and Forward work all the way to the start of the game, also after closing the page.
- Endings Gallery with remembered players.
- Help pages and the Log Book, now for all three scenarios.
- SPECIAL RULE blocks get their own pop-up.

## v0.66.6.8 - v0.66.6.16 - 27 to 29 October 2026

- Oliver narrates Fear of the Unknown; fixes from the first table play tests.

## v0.66.6.0 - v0.66.6.7 - 13 to 17 October 2026

- Music, sound effects, the original male and female voice-over and page transitions.
- A Time of War fully ported, with its music and voice-over.
- Version numbers.

## Before version numbers - September and October 2026

- The Cost of Disease Generation III, final scoring, tie-breakers and all 8 endings.
- Fear of the Unknown fully ported.
- Play along with the official app.
- Published on GitHub Pages.
- Built on Deusald's fan-made app, which ported the first two Generations of The Cost of Disease (July 2026).
